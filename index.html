Official Rules

I. Tournament Overview

  • Participation — 6 teams total. Cross-school combined teams are permitted; eligibility is limited to students currently enrolled at international schools.
  • Schedule — Round-robin stage: 20 & 22 July, every team plays every other team once. The Final: 24 July, the top two teams meet to decide the championship.

II. Registration & Eligibility

Every team must complete all of the following before the stated deadlines; failure to do so will be treated as voluntary withdrawal.

  • Entry fee
    • ¥500.00 (RMB 500) per team. No limit on roster size.
    • Due by 12:00 noon (Beijing time), 18 July.
    • Payable through the designated channel in the “Guangzhou International School Basketball League Team Managers” group.
  • Uniforms
    • Each team prepares two sets of game jerseys in the same style (short-sleeved tees or vests).
    • One set must be dark and the other light, so the two teams are clearly distinguished on court.
    • Numbers must be clearly legible and meet FIBA requirements for size and placement.
  • Official roster
    • Full information for every player, in the format: name + mobile number (e.g., Zhang San, 13112345678).
    • Due by 12:00 noon (Beijing time), 18 July. Late submissions will not be accepted.
  • Personal accident insurance
    • Every player must purchase the “Sports Accident Insurance” offered on Alipay before play begins on 20 July.
    • At pre-game check-in, each player must present valid proof of insurance, or will not be permitted to play.

III. Rules & Officiating

  • The tournament is played under the FIBA 2026 Official Basketball Rules and their latest official interpretations.
  • All rulings are at the final discretion of the referees; teams and players must accept them unconditionally — no objections or protests.

IV. Round-Robin Stage — 20 & 22 July

  • Format — single round-robin: each team plays every other team once — 15 games in total.
  • Game length — two 12-minute halves (running clock), 24 minutes total.
  • Points system
    • Win: 2 points. Draw: 1 point. Loss: 0 points.
    • Standings by points; where tied, in order: head-to-head result; head-to-head point differential; total point differential (further tie-breaks per FIBA rules).
  • Advancement — the top two teams at the end of the round-robin advance to the final; third place goes to the team ranked third in the standings.
  • Each team’s fixtures follow the published schedule.

V. The Final — 24 July

  • Matchup — the top two teams from the round-robin meet in a single final to decide the championship.
  • Game length — four 10-minute quarters (running clock), except the last 2 minutes of the fourth quarter and any overtime, played with a stopped clock.
  • Result — the team with the higher score at the end of regulation wins.

VI. Overtime & Tie-Breaks

  • If the final is tied at the end of regulation, a 5-minute overtime is played (running clock).
  • If still tied after overtime, the match is decided by free throws per FIBA rules until a winner is determined.

08 发展愿景

从一场赛事，
到一整个赛季。

从明年起，联赛将从暑期单届赛事，升级为「常规赛 + 季后赛」的系列赛架构：在周末进行、拉长战线、吸纳更多队伍，经过数十场比赛后争夺总冠军。

  1. 2026 首届联赛落地（6 队 · 15 场）
  2. 2027 常规赛 + 季后赛系列赛
  3. 未来 更多运动 —— 橄榄球、排球联赛
  4. 长期 大型校际社区与资源共享
